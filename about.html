About Us

4R BET Support Center ek helpdesk hai jahan aap deposit, withdrawal, email verification aur identity verification se judi har problem aaram se bata sakte hain. Yahan aapko sirf form nahi milta — aapki complaint ka poora record, uska status aur live chat support bhi milta hai, taaki aapko baar-baar dobara samjhana na pade.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1 Hum kaun hain

  • 1 4R BET ka official support helpdesk. Hamara kaam sirf ek hai — aapki problem ko jitni jaldi ho sake solve karwana, bina aapko kisi third person ke chakkar me bheje.
  • 2 Poora support ek hi jagah. Deposit, withdrawal, email verification, identity verification aur live chat — sab kuch isi website par, alag-alag app ya link dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • 3 Har complaint ka record safe rehta hai. Ek baar complaint bhejne ke baad wo aapke Mailbox me dikhti hai — apni complaint ID, date, time aur current status ke saath.
  • 4 Team aapko update deti rehti hai. Jab bhi aapki complaint par kaam aage badhta hai, uska status badal jata hai — Pending se Previewing, aur phir Successful.

2 Hum kis-kis cheez me madad karte hain

Deposit Problem

Paisa cut gaya lekin account me nahi aaya, deposit pending hai, ya payment screenshot ke saath complaint karni ho — sab yahan se.

Withdrawal Problem

Withdrawal request pending hai, amount account me nahi aaya, ya bank/UPI details galat lag rahi hain — turant complaint darj karein.

Email Verification

Verification link inbox me nahi aaya, code expire ho gaya, ya email galat type ho gaya — hum dobara verify karwa dete hain.

Identity Verification

ID proof submit nahi ho raha, document reject ho gaya, ya photo clear nahi hai — guide karke dobara submit karwate hain.

Online Chat

Jab form se kaam na bane ya jaldi jawab chahiye — seedha agent se chat karein, text, photo ya file ke saath.

Har complaint ka status

Mailbox me Total, Pending, Previewing aur Successful ka hisaab hamesha samne rehta hai — kuch bhi chhup nahi rehta.

3 Complain kaise kaam karti hai (4 aasan step)

  • 1 Problem chuno. Home page par jo problem aapki hai wahi card kholiye — deposit, withdrawal, email ya identity.
  • 2 Form bharo. User name, mobile, email, amount ya problem ki detail likhiye. Jahan screenshot/photo maanga hai, wahan saaf dikhne wali image lagaiye.
  • 3 Complaint ID milegi. Submit karte hi aapki complaint save ho jati hai aur ek complaint ID banti hai. Wo ID aapke Mailbox me turant dikhne lagti hai.
  • 4 Status check karte raho. Mailbox kholiye — status update hote hote Successful par aa jayega. Madad chahiye to Online Chat se baat kar lijiye.

4 Status ka matlab kya hai

  • Pending — aapki complaint team tak pahunch gayi hai, line me hai. Is step par zyada tar complaints apne aap settle ho jati hain.
  • Previewing — team aapki complaint par kaam kar rahi hai. Screenshots check ho rahe hain ya details verify ho rahi hain. Is time par reminder bhejne ki zaroorat nahi hoti.
  • Successful — kaam poora ho gaya. Deposit add ho gaya, withdrawal release ho gaya, ya verification approve ho gaya — uske baad Mailbox me status badal jayega.
Zyada tar cases 15 se 30 minute ke andar settle ho jate hain. Agar aapki complaint jaanch ke liye lambi hai, to status Previewing hi rahega — bas thoda intezaar karein aur ek hi problem par baar-baar nayi complaint na banayein.

5 Aapki safety hamari zimmedari

  • 1 Sirf isi website par detail daaliye. Login detail ya screenshot kisi WhatsApp group, Telegram channel ya kisi ajnabi link par kabhi na bhejein. Official support sirf yahin hai.
  • 2 Koi bhi paisa nahi maangta. Support ka koi charge nahi hai. Agar koi "jaldi karwana hai to pay karo" bole — wo fraud hai, turant report karein.
  • 3 Complaint ID sambhal ke rakhiye. Chat me baat karte waqt complaint ID batane se kaam turant aage badhta hai.
  • 4 Sahi detail hi sahi result deti hai. Galat mobile number ya galat amount likhne se complaint reject ho sakti hai aur time waste hota hai.

6 Support timing aur reach

  • Online Chat: 24 x 7 khula rehta hai — jab bhi zaroorat ho, baat karein.
  • Complaint forms: din ke kisi bhi time bhar sakte hain, record turant ban jata hai.
  • Jawab dene ka time: aam taur par 15–30 minute, busy time me thoda zyada.
  • Bhasha: Hindi, Hinglish aur English — jis bhasha me aaram ho usi me likhiye.
