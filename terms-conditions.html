Terms & Conditions

Ye terms is support website (4R BET Support Center) ke use ke liye hain. Jab aap yahan koi complaint bhejte hain ya online chat use karte hain, to aap in baaton se sehmat hote hain. Neeche sab kuch aasan bhasha me, point-wise likha gaya hai — ek baar zaroor padh lijiye.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1 Terms ki is website par lagti hain

  • Ye support page 4R BET users ki madad ke liye banaya gaya hai — deposit, withdrawal, email verification, identity verification aur live chat.
  • Complaint bhejne ke liye account banana zaroori nahi. Aapki details bas aapki complaint ke record ke liye use hoti hain.
  • Har aapke request par team ka final decision legal aur safety rules ke hisaab se hota hai.

2 Jo information aap dete hain

  • 1 Detail sahi aur poori honi chahiye. User name, mobile number, email, amount aur problem ki detail bilkul wahi honi chahiye jo aapke account me hai. Galat detail se complaint reject ho sakti hai ya late ho sakti hai.
  • 2 Login detail sirf isi form me daaliye. Password sirf verification ke liye hota hai aur form me daalna optional bhi ho sakta hai. Ise kisi WhatsApp, Telegram ya ajnabi link par kabhi na bhejein.
  • 3 Documents aur screenshots aapke apne hone chahiye. Kisi doosre ki ID, screenshot ya bank detail upload na karein. Aisa karne par complaint band kar di jayegi.
  • 4 Ek problem par ek hi complaint. Baar-baar same complaint bhejne se aapka case slow hota hai, turant solve nahi hota. Status check karne ke liye Mailbox dekhiye.

3 Complaint ka process aur time

  • 1 Submit karte hi record ban jata hai. Aapki complaint ek complaint ID ke saath save hoti hai aur Mailbox me dikhti hai — chahe internet slow ho.
  • 2 Status: Pending → Previewing → Successful. Pending matlab line me hai, Previewing matlab team kaam kar rahi hai, Successful matlab kaam poora ho gaya.
  • 3 Normal time: 15 se 30 minute. Jaanch wale case (jaise ID verification ya bada withdrawal) me thoda zyada time lag sakta hai.
  • 4 Kuch bhi extra charge nahi. Support free hai. Paisa maangne wala koi bhi message fraud hai — use ignore karein aur yahan report karein.

4 Kya allowed nahi hai

  • Fake complaint ya jhoothi detail dena — jaan-boojh kar amount badha kar likhna, doosre ka account detail dena.
  • Doosre ki photo, ID ya document bina ijazat upload karna.
  • Chat me gaali-galoch, dhamki, ya agent se badtameezi karna.
  • Baar-baar spam complaint bhejna ya bot/tool se form bharna.
  • Website ko hack, copy ya damage karne ki koshish karna.
Dhyan dijiye: upar likhi koi bhi cheez karne par aapki complaint bina notice band ho sakti hai, aur aage support lene par rok lag sakti hai.

5 Aapka data kaise rakha jata hai

  • 1 Complaint aur chat record cloud me save hota hai. Isse team kisi bhi device se aapki complaint dekh sakti hai aur record delete nahi hota.
  • 2 Aapke device par ek copy bhi rehti hai. Isi wajah se internet na hone par bhi aapki complaint save rehti hai aur Mailbox me dikhti hai.
  • 3 Complaint sirf support team dekhti hai. Aapka record kisi third party ko becha ya share nahi kiya jata. (Bina matlab ke kisi ko apni detail bhejna aapki apni zimmedari hai.)
  • 4 Browsing data clear karne par device copy hat jati hai. Isliye apni complaint ID kahin note kar lijiye — usi se team aapki complaint dhoondh sakti hai.

6 Result aur zimmedari

  • Team apni taraf se poori koshish karti hai, lekin har complaint ka result same nahi hota — kuch cases rules ke hisaab se reject bhi ho sakte hain.
  • Galat detail, adhoora screenshot ya fake document ki wajah se hone wale nuksan ki zimmedari user ki hogi.
  • Complaint ka status hi official jawab hai — kisi bhi agent ke personal message ko official na maane.

7 Terms me change

  • Zaroorat padne par ye terms update ho sakti hain. Upar "Last updated" ki date se pata chalega ki kab badla.
  • Website ka use jaari rakhne ka matlab hai ki aap naye terms se sehmat hain.
  • Koi sawal ho to Online Chat se poochh lijiye — team aasan bhasha me samjha degi.

Terms padh liye? Ab aaram se complaint bhej dijiye.
